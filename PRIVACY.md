# Privacy Policy — احتساب ناصبیت (Ehtisab e Nasibiyat)

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

This app lets you read the book *Ehtisab e Nasibiyat* on your phone.

**What the app collects:** nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking,
and it does not send any personal information anywhere.

**What stays on your phone:** your bookmarks, bookmark notes, the last page you read in each volume,
your night-mode setting, and the volumes you download. All of this is stored only on your device and
is removed when you uninstall the app.

**Internet use:** the app connects to the internet only when you choose to download a volume.
The files are downloaded from GitHub (github.com), which, like any web server, receives your IP
address as part of the connection. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.

**Questions:** open an issue at https://github.com/baqirmuhammad/ehtisab-e-nasibiyat-books/issues
